🃏 Privacy Policy — Yaniv Score Tracker

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Overview

Yaniv Score Tracker ("the App") is a free, offline score-keeping application for the Yaniv card game. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App collects (spoiler: none).

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. The App:

  • Does not require an account or login
  • Does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information
  • Does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services
  • Does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or any other device sensors
  • Does not connect to any external servers

Local Storage

All game data (player names, scores, game history) is stored locally on your device using your browser's local storage. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not accessible to us or any third party
  • Can be cleared at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings

Sharing Results

If you tap Share, the App creates an image of the final scores on your device and hands it to the app you choose (for example a messaging app). Nothing is sent unless you choose to share, and what happens next is governed by that app's privacy policy.

Internet Permission

The App requests the INTERNET permission solely for the Capacitor framework's internal operation. No user data is transmitted over the network.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. It is safe for all ages.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs that collect user data.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, the changes will be reflected here with an updated date. Since the App collects no data, significant changes are unlikely.
